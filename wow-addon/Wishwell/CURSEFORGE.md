# Wishwell Forever

A loot database for **WoW Forever**, in the game. See what drops anywhere, try it on your character, mark what you want, and get told the moment it drops. Works solo, at any level.

## What it does

**What next?**
- One screen that answers "what should I do now?": the best quest where you are, where the most quest XP is waiting, what your spells cost and whether you can afford them, and a dungeon for your level with how many upgrades it holds for you.

**Upgrade tips everywhere**
- Any item tooltip in the game says whether the item is an upgrade for you, what changes, and why: which gains and losses matter for your class.
- A loot roll on an upgrade gets a chat line. A quest with a choice of rewards gets the best one labelled.

**Loot**
- Every raid and dungeon in WoW Forever, including the new ones. Pick a place and a boss, or type to search everything at once.
- Filter by zone, class, rarity and slot. Item names use the game's rarity colours.
- The list jumps to the dungeon you walk into.

**Drop rates**
- See how often each item drops and how many runs that means. Wishwell also counts the boss kills it sees, so the numbers get better the more you play.
- What next? names the run with the best odds of a wishlist drop.

**Character preview**
- Click any item to see it on your own character. Drag to turn. **Reset** puts your real gear back.

**Wishlist**
- Click **Wish** on anything you want.
- When a wishlist item drops or comes up for a roll, you get a message on screen, a sound and a chat line.
- When you loot it, it comes off the list.

**Is it an upgrade?**
- Items better than what you are wearing are marked **Upgrade**, and the best one for each slot **Best upgrade**.
- Hover an item to see exactly which stats you would gain and lose against your current piece.
- Tick **Upgrades first** to sort by how much each item improves your gear.

**Item sets**
- 197 sets: Classic tier, dungeon and PvP sets, plus the new WoW Forever sets.
- Click a set to put the whole thing on your character and see its set bonuses. **Wish all** adds every piece.

**Quests**
- Every Classic quest your character can do, by zone, sorted by the XP it gives at your level, so you always know the best use of your time.
- Class-only and race-only quests are marked. Quests you have outlevelled show the smaller XP they would really pay.
- Plan ahead: see what each locked quest is waiting for.

**Spell training**
- Visit your class trainer once. Wishwell then shows every spell you have coming up, level by level, with what each level will cost you.
- On level-up, chat tells you how many new spells there are and the total.

**Gold goals and characters**
- Set what you are saving for and watch your progress, with an estimate of how long is left.
- A Characters tab shows the level, XP to next level, gold, wishlist and training of every character on your account.

**Professions**
- Open a profession window once and Wishwell lists the recipes that will raise your skill, with their materials and how many you can make right now.

**Group wishlists**
- In a dungeon, see what your group members want (and they see yours), so nobody rolls against a friend by accident.

**Legacy**
- Your Legacy challenges and points on one tab, with the challenges you are closest to finishing at the top.

**Run summary**
- Leave a dungeon and see what the run gave you: bosses down, wishlist items won or missed, new drops learned.

**Level-up pop-up and tips**
- When you level, the wisp shows what just opened up: new spells and their cost, new quests, a dungeon now in range.
- New to the game? The wisp explains loot rolls, reward choices and trainers the first time you meet them.

**Dungeon pop-up**
- Walk into a raid or dungeon and a small pop-up shows what drops there for your class: wishlist items first, then upgrades. A little wisp keeps it company.

## How to use

Type `/ww`, or click the minimap button.

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/ww` | Open / close the window |
| `/ww next` | Open on the What next? tab |
| `/ww loot` | Open on the Loot tab |
| `/ww sets` | Open on the Item sets tab |
| `/ww train` | Open on the Spell training tab |
| `/ww quests` | Open on the Quests tab |
| `/ww clear` | Empty your wishlist (asks first) |
| `/ww popup` | Turn the dungeon pop-up on or off |
| `/ww wisp` | Hide or show the wisp |
| `/ww prof` | Open on the Professions tab |
| `/ww legacy` | Open on the Legacy tab |
| `/ww settings` | Open the settings |
| `/ww pins` | Put your pinned recipes away, or bring them back |
| `/ww link` | Put your wishlist in the chat box |
| `/ww alts` | Open on the Characters tab |
| `/ww goal 100 Epic mount` | Set a gold goal |
| `/ww share` | Turn group wishlist sharing on or off |
| `/ww tips` | Turn upgrade tips on tooltips, rolls and quest rewards on or off |
| `/ww hints` | Turn the wisp's first-time tips on or off |
| `/ww sound` | Turn the alert sound on or off |
| `/ww minimap` | Hide or show the minimap button |

## Good to know

- **The loot list grows by itself.** Loot nobody has seen in WoW Forever yet is marked "Classic loot, not confirmed". Wishwell remembers what it sees drop and shares it with group members who also have it. A raid or dungeon it has never heard of is added the first time you step inside.
- **The new raids start empty.** Blizzard has not published their loot. They fill in as bosses die.
- **The quest list starts as Classic's.** WoW Forever keeps the Classic quests. Its new quests are added as you pick them up.
- **The upgrade verdict is a guide.** The stat differences are the game's own numbers; "upgrade" is a rule of thumb for what each class usually values, not a simulation.
- Wishwell never rolls, loots or equips anything for you.
